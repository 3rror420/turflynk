MowNWA Data Deletion Instructions

MowNWA.com respects your privacy. You can request deletion of personal data associated with your MowNWA account, including data connected through Facebook Login.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your data, email us at:

Email: support@mownwa.com

Please include the email address or name associated with your MowNWA account so we can locate your records.

Facebook Login users

If you used Facebook Login with MowNWA, you can also remove MowNWA from your Facebook account by following these steps:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Apps and Websites.
  5. Find MowNWA.
  6. Remove the app and choose the option to request deletion of your data.

What we delete

After receiving your request, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, including Facebook Login data if applicable.

Retention

Some limited information may be retained when required for legal, billing, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or service-record purposes.

Confirmation

After your request is received, we will process it and may contact you if additional information is needed to identify your account.
