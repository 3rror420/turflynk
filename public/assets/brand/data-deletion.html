User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: April 28, 2026

You can request deletion of data associated with your MowNWA account and Facebook Login. This may include account/login information, contact information you submitted, and service request details connected to your account.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Go to MowNWA.com.
  2. Contact us through the website or email support if available.
  3. Include the email address used with Facebook Login.
  4. Request "Delete my MowNWA account and Facebook Login data".

Remove MowNWA from Facebook

  1. Go to Facebook Settings.
  2. Open Apps and Websites.
  3. Find MowNWA.
  4. Remove the app.

After verification, MowNWA will delete or anonymize account/login data unless retention is required for legal, security, billing, or service-record reasons.
