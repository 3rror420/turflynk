How the editor works

  1. Enter address and click Find Address then Lookup Parcel.
  2. Green outline shows the parcel boundary.
  3. Click Lasso Yard, then click the map once to start drawing.
  4. Move your mouse (or finger) around the mowable area to trace it.
  5. Click again to finish — the polygon closes automatically.
  6. Drag any vertex handle to adjust the shape. Area updates live.
  7. Use Cut Out Area to remove driveways, buildings, etc.
  8. Click Clear Areas and lasso again to start over.
Touchscreen tip Tap Lasso Yard, then tap-and-trace around the yard. Tap again to close. Vertex handles appear for fine-tuning.
