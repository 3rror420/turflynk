Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026

MowNWA.com helps customers get lawn care estimates and submit service requests. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, and phone number if you submit them.
  • Service address, location details, parcel information, and map data used for estimates.
  • Quote details, service request information, access notes, photos, and scheduling preferences you provide.
  • Login/account information from Facebook Login or other account tools you choose to use.

How We Use Information

  • To provide lawn care quotes and service request workflows.
  • To create and manage customer login/account sessions.
  • To communicate about quotes, bookings, scheduling, payments, and support.
  • To improve map, parcel, estimate, and service request features.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Facebook Login, Google Maps/Places, map and parcel data providers, hosting providers, analytics tools, and payment processors when payments are used. These services may process information under their own policies.

No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on MowNWA.com.
