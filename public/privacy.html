Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026

MowNWA.com helps customers get lawn care estimates and submit service requests. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we handle SMS/text messaging information.

Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, and phone number if you submit them.
  • Service address, location details, parcel information, and map data used for estimates.
  • Quote details, service request information, access notes, photos, and scheduling preferences you provide.
  • Login/account information from Facebook Login or other account tools you choose to use.
  • SMS/text message consent records, message preferences, and communication history related to quotes, bookings, scheduling, payment status, and customer support.

How We Use Information

  • To provide lawn care quotes and service request workflows.
  • To create and manage customer login/account sessions.
  • To communicate about quotes, bookings, scheduling, payments, service updates, and support.
  • To send SMS/text messages only when you have provided consent or when messages are related to a service request you started.
  • To improve map, parcel, estimate, and service request features.

SMS/Text Messaging

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you agree to receive SMS/text messages from MowNWA.com related to lawn care quotes, booking requests, scheduling, job updates, payment or cash-on-delivery verification, and customer support.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us through the contact options on MowNWA.com.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in consent, or text messaging data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Facebook Login, Google Maps/Places, map and parcel data providers, hosting providers, analytics tools, SMS/text messaging providers, and payment processors when payments are used. These services may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and may be subject to their own policies.

No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we collect. However, no website, payment system, or communication method is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request help, update your information, or ask questions about your information through the contact options on MowNWA.com. You may opt out of SMS/text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on MowNWA.com.
